# P01 — Checklist

Before leaving this phase:

- [ ] I wrote my own pick and reasoning **before** the chat weighed in
- [ ] The chosen idea is one paragraph, in my words, and I can say it out loud
- [ ] I can name the audience and what they'll attack first
- [ ] The idea is buildable with data I actually have access to

- [ ] Parked ideas went to the backlog, not the rejection log
- [ ] If this extends prior work, `LINEAGE.md` exists or is planned
