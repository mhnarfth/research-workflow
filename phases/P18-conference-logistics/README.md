# P18 — Conference Logistics

**Triggered at acceptance.** Its own phase because it has real deadlines that
have nothing to do with the paper, and they arrive early.

## Visa and travel first

For international travel this is the **longest pole and entirely outside your
control.** Start the day you're accepted, not when the flights get expensive.

Check whether you need an invitation letter from the organizers — most
conferences issue them, and most take a week or two.

## Order of operations

1. **Visa / travel documents** — start immediately
2. **University travel request submitted and approved** — this comes before
   booking anything, and approval takes time
3. **Registration** — note the early-bird date; the difference is real money
4. **Student travel grants** — many venues offer them and the deadlines are
   early. Also check your department, the society, and the NSF
5. **Flights, accommodation, ground transport** — after the travel request is
   approved
6. **Know the reimbursement rules before you travel** — which receipts, what
   documentation, per-diem or actuals. Finding out afterward is how money gets
   lost
7. **After you return: expense report with all receipts.** Easy to forget, and
   it's real money

## Index

Record on the paper's row: conference dates, registration deadline, travel
funding status, visa status.
